# 空间意象 / Spatial Imagery — launch film

Original 30-second bilingual SDK film. Working title: **Give ideas room to move / 让想法成为空间**.

## Direction and research

Three directions considered: typographic code manifesto (fast but weak evidence of depth); intimate materials/assembly (selected); cosmic scale journey (poor fit for a general-purpose SDK). Warm paper, midnight enamel, sea-glass and vermilion establish a material vocabulary tied to the design table. No galaxy, stock neon particles or dashboard montage.

Research: [BUCK / GitHub](https://buck.co/work/github-what-is-github) for a recognizable subject connecting mixed media; [Oddfellows / Adobe Foundations](https://oddfellows.tv/work/adobe-foundations) for design principles expressed by transformations; [FutureDeluxe / Huawei](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/huawei) for raw material becoming a functional object; [Monotype / Helvetica Now](https://www.monotype.com/fonts/helvetica-now) for restrained optical-scale typography. These are reference pages, not licensed footage or completed playback reviews. No third-party film frames or soundtracks are reused.

Runtime: original Blender geometry driven by **SDK-generated absolute frame poses**, Remotion typography/video composition, FFmpeg encoding/audio. HyperFrames was considered for typography but is unnecessary for this existing Remotion pipeline. The current user authorized production and publication; no historical OpenMontage approvals are inherited or re-imposed. No paid generation, cost $0.

## One connected design table

| SH / time | Subject and keyword | Initial → action → result | Camera / handoff | Sound / reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SH01 / 0–4 s | EL01 vermilion marked sheet; “先有主体 / Start with a subject” | Dispersed design pages gather; marked sheet lifts above them | Establish the object, move toward it; same mesh continues | Soft material movement; brief contact; title at 0.8–3.5 s |
| SH02 / 4–11 s | EL01; “主体带路 / Follow the subject” | Sheet travels across five frames at different depths | Follow at a consistent offset; foreground occlusion reveals actual depth | Movement body follows SDK velocity; gradual rise, pass, deceleration; 0.8 s fade-in, 1 s fade-out |
| SH03 / 11–20 s | EL02 layered assembly receives EL01; “镜头揭示关系 / Reveal the relationship” | Sheet lands; story/subject/camera/sound/assets layers separate, then assemble | Continuous camera pullout and arc; distinguish same mesh from newly revealed assembly | Material change crossfade into mechanical motion; separate contact event; hold at 18–20 s |
| SH04 / 20–25 s | VID01 pink ink in water; “素材进入叙事 / Bring footage into the story” | Moving ink fills a frame; moving video recedes into a composition surface | Deliberate cut at 20 s; movement continues inside the surface; video freezes only after its last used frame | Short transition, soft motion; source muted; 3 s readable phrase |
| SH05 / 25–30 s | Original brand mark and bilingual name | Composition resolves into a spatial frame mark; title settles | Clean landing with stable reading; no claim of physical mesh continuity with stock footage | Decay, final gentle contact, deliberate quiet at end |

All intervals are half-open. 30 fps, 900 frames, 1600×900. Large bilingual typography remains an overlay, not a 3D object. No A/B talking-head state is applicable to this no-presenter film. This example demonstrates choreography and production records; it does not claim automatic transcription, matting or human relighting.

## Sources and sound plan

Actively searched footage terms: ink water abstract; pink ink cloud. Selected [Mixkit 41992](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/pink-ink-cloud-entering-water-41992/), explicitly marked Stock Video Free License. Raw footage stays outside the public package; downloader and source/hash manifest identify it. Incorporation in the edited film only. Rejected green-ink 48472 because its free download is personal-use only.

Sound search began before mixing: movement/engine, soft contact, glass/mechanical opening. CC0 Kenney sources are included with a per-file manifest and attribution. They are designed sound effects, not claimed as recordings of these physical objects. No generated noise bed, no copied commercial soundtrack. Music is optional in this SDK; the first launch-film mix uses designed motion sound to preserve clear material transitions.

## Review contract

Check software tests, reproducible frame poses, key frames and handoffs, stock-video motion, decoded frame count and durations, final audio peak/fades. Technical checks, sampled visual review, normal-speed playback and subjective listening are separate fields in `qa.json`. User approval is not presumed.
