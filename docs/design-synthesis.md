# Designing from intent, before building scenes

[简体中文](design-synthesis.zh-CN.md) · Part of [the production workflow](pipeline.md). Workflow prose: CC BY 4.0; see [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).

Use this method for a new film or a substantial visual redesign. A local technical repair keeps the accepted design. It supplies creative decisions for the existing SH/EL/TR records, not another approval layer or a new JSON schema. SDK functions and gate validators do not invent or certify good design by themselves.

Read [design principles](design-principles.md) first for the causal reasoning behind imagery, recognition, staging, dimensional expression, camera work, movement, rhythm, typography and visual unity. Apply a principle to a concrete decision and expected perception; copying its name is not a design rationale.

## Own the creative decisions

Given the subject, script, music or visual direction, the maker proposes and selects imagery, transformations and camera work. Ask only for missing facts, access, authorization or preferences that materially change the result. Do not require the user to invent each transition. Develop an explicit user idea directly; do not reopen it as a multiple-choice question.

Spatial Imagery supports 2D, layered 2.5D, 3D, typography and footage. Choose them for what they express. Neither the pipeline name nor a previous example requires a fully modeled environment. Mixed dimensions need visible transitions when requested, not a compulsory ratio or cycle in every film. A/B belongs only to talking-head work; lyric and motion-design films use general/full.

## Derive imagery from relationships

Read the actual words and their uncertainty, emotional direction and timing. With music, use real phrases, accents, sustained notes and rests. Without verified audio timing, describe relative phases instead of inventing timestamps. Do not turn every noun into a modeled scene.

State the observable idea: approaching but still apart; disorder becoming organized; a vision expanding and returning to its speaker. Poetic imagery may remain ambiguous, but waiting must not accidentally become reunion, or an aspiration an accomplished fact.

List what candidate subjects can change: outline, boundary, parts, material behavior, function, motion and spatial relationship. A gap can become a meaningful negative shape; a trace can become a path or letterform; a close-up can later reveal itself as part of a larger object. These are search directions, not fixed effects assigned to emotions.

For an unresolved narrative or important interface, compare genuinely different paths and select one. Differences must concern meaning, behavior or viewing order, not just colors. Each candidate states:

> Content/emotion → subject and changeable property → trigger → visible intermediate state → next subject/state → resulting meaning or feeling.

Reject candidates with no content reason, no readable landing, no describable intermediate state, or an unworkable core mechanism. Record the selected path, the important rejected alternative and the remaining prototype question in the one-page brief. No quota of proposals is needed. Existing approved directions remain valid.

When research is missing, inspect relevant ranges of complete creator films and approved studies. Record the actual motion/camera mechanism and its adaptation. A screenshot, tool name or repository star count is not a design rationale. Keep the workflow's footage and sound searches; a concept description does not replace them.

## Specify the intermediate state

In EL/TR, record the starting state and dimension, trigger, preserved outline/part/position/momentum, intermediate form, ending state and dimension, and attention takeover. Distinguish same-object transformation, new-subject relay, occlusion editing and composition matching. A dissolve between unrelated meshes is not a continuous mesh morph.

For mixed dimensions, also state:

- What the flat stage expresses through graphics, type, outlines, layout or planar space. A toon shader on an otherwise unchanged 3D model does not establish a promised graphic conversion.
- What reveals depth: a lifting/folding surface, an exposed side, parallax, occlusion or useful lighting. Select the cues the shot needs.
- The screen anchor during conversion: outline, position, scale, direction and momentum. Check projection/camera changes for jumps.
- What is actually implemented with paths, compositing, meshes, shaders or editing. Name 2D/2.5D/3D honestly. A controlled cloth deformation need not be a costly physical simulation, but an implementation shortcut cannot silently remove the core action.

When a literal scene is impractical, preserve the relationship, handoff and landing with a controllable expression. For example, typographic grouping and empty space can express separation. Repeated static lyric cards cannot substitute for a promised animation. Explain material changes to accepted designs.

## Design the camera backward from the discovery

First identify the relationship the viewer must see at the end. Choose its readable framing and angle; work backward to a view where it is not yet revealed, and identify the subject that leads attention there. Do not finish the model and then add an arbitrary orbit.

Each SH/TR answers: **first subject → attention cue → change of viewpoint → discovery → readable landing → next subject**.

| Task | Possible design | Failure to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Read a shape changing | Hold or gently reframe while the subject performs | Camera movement obscures the transformation |
| Discover a plane gaining volume | Move from frontal/overhead to an angle that reveals the side, preserving its anchor | Rotation reveals no new depth or relationship |
| Discover the function of a detail | Preserve the identifying outline, then pull back or pass a reference object | The result is too small or still unclear |
| Follow a subject into the next image | Track, reveal the next target before takeover, then settle | Endless travel with no time to read the result |
| Return from space to lyrics | Align with a meaningful contour and settle into a legible planar composition | Strong perspective, blur or rotation persists through reading |

These are reasoning examples, not a camera menu. Motivated cuts, stable shots and multiple viewpoints are valid. A long take is not a quality requirement. Cuts cannot conceal a core transformation promised in the design.

## Let lyrics act as imagery

Lyrics can be readable text, expressive letterforms/layout, or the source of another image. Design how the previous subject becomes strokes, rows or negative space; provide an accurate and legible reading window; then choose a content-relevant stroke, outline or layout relationship to generate the next subject. Match typography, material, palette and motion to the rest of the film.

Time anticipation, transformation, reveal, reading and release to actual musical phrases. They may overlap with a clear hierarchy. Do not trigger a new effect on every beat or accelerate the song to fit the animation. Check whether the music already supplies an accent or sustained energy before selecting process/contact effects. Sound follows inertia and material handoffs, with intentional quiet where useful. Record real source selection and listening; a continuous noise bed is not continuity.

## Prototype and critique the design

Before expensive detail work, make a readable motion draft of the hardest interface and its context: both subjects, the real intermediate state, the camera discovery, landing and sound timing. Then repair the viewing relationship before raising geometry or render quality. A beautiful opening frame cannot stand in for this prototype.

Use existing G1 criteria, including intent, specificity, visible_process, camera_discovery, reading_rhythm and reference_adaptation, plus the whole-film critique. G2/G3 inspect the actual film. This guidance introduces no new machine-validated fields and cannot make the checker judge taste.

Actively look for counterexamples:

- Would an unrelated topic need only new titles? Rework the subject's relationship and behavior. When type is the subject, retain the text and inspect how its behavior expresses that content.
- Would fixing the camera lose none of the intended discovery, emotional or rhythmic effect? Remove meaningless movement or redesign the task; a stable camera can be correct and an emotional move need not reveal a new fact.
- Would removing the conversion preserve the same intended meaning and feeling? Check whether the conversion has a role before making it more spectacular.
- Does a requested 2D/3D sequence contain only travel through 3D sets? Repair the missing dimensional interface.
- Do difficult passages all become the same lyric card, or does only the opening develop? Repair those passages and their handoffs.

Write observed outcomes and defects, not these questions copied into a pass report. Text review, dynamic viewing, listening, technical verification and user approval remain different evidence.

The user-proposed petal → flat ripple → cloth → lyric sequence belongs to the *Shuangxue Qiannian* brief. Reuse the reasoning about shape, momentum and discovery; do not turn petals, water, cloth, ancient-Chinese styling or dimensional cycling into a template for other films.
